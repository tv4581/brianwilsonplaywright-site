About

Brian Wilson is a playwright based in Rock Hill, South Carolina, writing character-driven dramas for the stage — from ten-minute one-acts to full-length works.

His play Introductions and Goodbyes is being produced this month as part of the Chapin Theatre Company's 6th Annual 10 Minute-ish Play Festival. His other plays include the full-length Alone Together Forever, along with a growing catalog of one-act comedies and dramas exploring family, faith, grief, and the stories people tell to survive them.

Plays

Alone Together Forever

Full-length · A Play in Two Acts

When a husband's shooting leaves him silent, and his wife's quiet betrayals surface one object at a time, a marriage vowed as one union comes apart into two people who never stopped carrying the broken families that shaped them.

Introductions and Goodbyes

One-Act

A treasured family cookbook and a nonagenarian grandmother's shady doings — in the end, a story about understanding, acceptance, and a final act of love.

Dearly Beloved

One-Act

A grieving man's funeral for his late partner becomes the target of a small-time con — a dark comedy about grief, hustle, and who really shows up when it counts.

Logged Out

One-Act Comedy · 8–10 minutes

A job interview conducted over a laptop turns into an accidental confession, as two blunt old friends refuse to let a man keep lying to himself.

Whole Again

One-Act

Six years after a quiet, devastating divorce, a chance encounter in a medical waiting room forces two ex-spouses to confront what they never said.

Gluten-Free Water

One-Act Comedy

A father reclaiming his garage workshop collides with his adult son's latest startup dream — a comedy about family, ambition, and knowing when to let go (of the garage).

4-F

One-Act · Period Comedy, 1966

Two teenagers scheme to flunk their draft physicals by any means necessary, while a third actually wants to go — a comedy about loyalty, cowardice, and growing up fast.

Lifetime Subscription

Short Two-Hander

A wry, tender two-hander about growing old together — and whether love outlasts the jokes you make about it.

Already Older

One-Act · approx. 12 minutes

A mother and daughter pack up the family home on moving day, arguing over what to do with an old photo album — until a voicemail from the mother's own aging mother turns a familiar old line about growing older into something neither woman expected: an offering.

Reflections

One-Character One-Act

Alone in the church he's swept for forty years, a recently widowed custodian revisits the interracial marriage that once scandalized his congregation, the neighborhood boys he mentored on the court outside, and a promise he made to raise a dying friend's son — a deal that might finally make him a father.

That Son-Of-A-Father!

One-Act · Four Scenes

When his father's life partner turns out to be his own exact bone-marrow match, an entitled adult son backs out to protect his inheritance — only to learn, in the reading of two wills, exactly what that choice cost him, and what it was always meant to teach him about fatherhood.

Prose

The Path Home

Short Story · Award Winner, York County Writer's Competition

A fictional dramatization drawn from real events in the author's life — a retired surgeon wakes to an unfamiliar morning, and an unfamiliar sense of who he is.
